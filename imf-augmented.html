How to read it. Each ring is sized so its area is proportional to its share of the total, and the two diagrams are scaled independently — debt is a stock, the deficit an annual flow. The two panels also build up along different paths, so each has its own stepper. Solid rings are official data; dashed rings are off-budget IMF staff estimates.

Definitions. Debt: central-government debt + explicit local-government debt is the official figure; the WEO / IMF general-government measure adds roughly two-thirds of estimated LGFV debt; the augmented measure adds the remaining LGFV debt plus government-guided and special-construction funds. Deficit: the general public budget headline balance, then the other general-government accounts (government-managed fund, social-insurance and SOE/cash) to reach the WEO / IMF general-government deficit, then off-budget LGFV, GGF and SCF spending for the augmented deficit. Source: IMF Country Report No. 26/44, Table 2.
