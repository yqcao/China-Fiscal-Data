China Local Government Bond Market Report
(July,2022)
Issued byChina Government Debt Center inAugust 2022
In July, the issuance of local government bonds (LGBs) were RMB406.31 billion, with a
month-on-month decrease of RMB1527.35 billion and a year-on-year decrease of
RMB250.47 billion. Average interest rate of issued LGBs was 2.97%, with a
month-on-month decrease of 11 bps and a year-on-year decrease of 34 bps. Average
maturity of issued LGBs was 9.72 years, with a month-on-month decrease of 5.05 years
and a year-on-year decrease of 1.87 years. The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the
secondary market was RMB719.27billion.
From January to July, the total issuance of LGBs were RMB5656.47 billion. Average
interest rate of issued LGBs was 3.07%, with a decrease of 35 bps year-on-year.Average
maturity of issued LGBs was 13.8 years, with a year-on-year increase of 3.21 years. The
amount ofspot transaction of LGBs inthe secondary market was RMB5651.69billion.

1.3 Interest Rate of Bond

and special bonds 3.06%. The average interest rate in July 2022 decreased by 11 bps
month-on-month and decreased by 34 bps year-on-year. The interest rate of issued LGBs
decreased month-on-month mainly because the average maturity of issued LGBs
decreased by5.05years month-on-month and the yields of bond market showed ageneral
downward trend in July. The trend of average interest rates of LGBs issued from January
2021 to July 2022 is shown in Figure 6. Average interest rates of LGBs issued in July
