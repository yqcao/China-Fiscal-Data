1.3 Interest Rate of Bond
In March 2023, average interest rate of issued LGBs was 3.05%, with general bonds
2.90% and special bonds 3.11%. The average interest rate in March 2023 decreased by 8
bps month-on-month and 9 bps year-on-year. Under the background of the People’s Bank
of China cutting the reserve requirement ratio in March and the average maturity of
issued LGBs decreasing nearly 2 years month-on-month, the interest rates of issued
LGBs decreased this month. The trend of average interest rates of LGBs issued from
January 2022 to March 2023 is shown in Figure 6.Average interest rates of LGBs issued
