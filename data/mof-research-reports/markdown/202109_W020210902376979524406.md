China Local Government Bond Market Report
(July, 2021)
Issued by China Government Debt Center in August 2021
In July, the issuance of local government bonds (LGBs) were RMB656.78 billion, with
a month-on-month decrease of RMB138.09 billion and a year-on-year increase of
RMB384.54 billion. Average maturity of issued LGBs was 11.59 years, with a month-
on-month decrease of 0.62 years and a year-on-year decrease of 2.75 years. Average
interest rate of issued LGBs was 3.31%, with a month-on-month decrease of 15 bps and
a year-on-year decrease of 9 bps. The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the
secondary market was RMB832.19 billion.
From January to July, the total issuance of LGBs were RMB3997.92 billion. Average
maturity of issued LGBs was 10.59 years, with a year-on-year decrease of 4.46 years.
Average interest rate of issued LGBs was 3.42%, with an increase of 14 bps year-on-
year. The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the secondary market was
RMB4473.57 billion.

1.3 Interest Rate of Bond
In July 2021, average interest rate of issued LGBs was 3.31%, with general bonds 3.12%
and special bonds 3.43%. The average interest rate in July 2021 decreased by 15 bps
month-on-month and 9 bps year-on-year. From January to June, the average interest
rate has fluctuated within a narrow range between 3.37% and 3.48%, mainly because
the monetary market interest rate has been relatively stable. The average interest rate
decreased significantly this month, mainly because the People's Bank of China
