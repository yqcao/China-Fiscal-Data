China Local Government Bond Market Report
(February, 2022)
Issued by China Government Debt Center in March 2022
In February, the issuance of local government bonds (LGBs) were RMB507.10 billion,
with a month-on-month decrease of RMB191.76 billion and a year-on-year increase of
RMB451.37 billion. Average interest rate of issued LGBs was 3.16%, with a month-
on-month increase of 11 bps and a year-on-year decrease of 29 bps. Average maturity
of issued LGBs was 15.07 years, with a month-on-month increase of 0.82 years and a
year-on-year increase of 6.2 years. The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the
secondary market was RMB677.94 billion.
From January to February, the total issuance of LGBs were RMB1205.95 billion.
Average interest rate of issued LGBs was 3.10%, with a decrease of 28 bps year-on-
year. Average maturity of issued LGBs was 14.6 years, with a year-on-year increase of
6.49 years. The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the secondary market was
RMB1789.47 billion.
