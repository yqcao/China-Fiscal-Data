1 Market Overview
1.1 Bond Issuance
In February 2020, 22 provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities in China
issued 123 local government bonds, worth a total of RMB437.92 billion.
By bond type, the issuance of general bonds were RMB202.95 billion and that of
special bonds were RMB234.97 billion.
By bond nature, all issued bonds were new bonds, worth a total of RMB437.92
billion.
By issuing place, all bonds were issued in the inter-bank market, worth a total of
RMB437.92 billion.

2 Balance of Local Government Bond
By the end of February 2020, the balance of Chinese local government bonds was
RMB22.34 trillion. In addition, the stock of local government debt (not in terms of
bond) was RMB188.93 billion. Therefore, the total balance of China’s local
government debt was RMB22.53 trillion.
By bond type, the balance of general bonds was RMB11.99 trillion and that of special
bonds was RMB10.35 trillion by the end of February 2020.
By maturity of bond, the average residual maturity of local government bonds was
5.38 years, 5.02 years for general bonds and 5.79 years for special bonds by the end of
February 2020.
In terms of interest rate, the average interest rate of local government bonds was
3.54%, with general bonds 3.54% and special bonds 3.53% by the end of February
2020.
