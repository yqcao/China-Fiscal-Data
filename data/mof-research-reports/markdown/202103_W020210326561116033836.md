China Local Government Bond Market Report
(February, 2021)
Issued by China Government Debt Center in March 2021
In February, the issuance of local government bonds (LGBs) were RMB55.72 billion,
with a decrease of RMB306.61 billion month-on-month and RMB382.20 billion year-
on-year. Average interest rate of issued LGBs was 3.45%, with an increase of 8 bps
month-on-month and 11 bps year-on-year. Average maturity of issued LGBs was 8.87
years, with a month-on-month increase of 0.88 years and a year-on-year decrease of
8.66 years. The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the secondary market was
RMB300.07 billion. From January to February, the total issuance of LGBs were
RMB418.06 billion. Average interest rate of issued LGBs was 3.38%, with a decrease
of 6 bps year-on-year. Average maturity of issued LGBs was 8.11 years, with a year-
on-year decrease of 7.06 years. The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the
secondary market was RMB684.09 billion.

3 Balance of Local Government Bond
By the end of February 2021, the balance of LGBs was RMB25.84 trillion, with general
bonds RMB12.80 trillion and special bonds RMB13.04 trillion. The average maturity
of outstanding LGBs was 6.7 years, which was 0.2 years shorter than that in the end of
2020. Among them, average maturity of general bonds was 6.1 years and special bonds
7.3 years. The average interest rate of LGBs was 3.51%, with general bonds 3.51% and
special bonds 3.50%.
The total balance of China’s local government debt was RMB26.02 trillion, including
LGBs and the stock of local government debt in the form of non-government bonds
(RMB175.06 billion).
