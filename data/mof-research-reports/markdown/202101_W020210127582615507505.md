1 Market Overview
1.1 Bond Issuance
In December 2020, 15 provinces, autonomous regions, municipalities in China issued
38 local government bonds (LGBs), worth a total of RMB183.61 billion, as is shown
in Table 1.
By bond type, the issuance of general bonds were RMB72.85 billion and that of special
bonds were RMB110.77 billion.
By bond nature, the issuance of new bonds were RMB58.03 billion and that of
refinancing bonds were RMB125.58 billion.
By issuing place, bonds issued in China Central Depository & Clearing Co., Ltd.
(CCDC) were RMB155.42 billion. Bonds issued on the Shanghai Stock Exchange were
RMB13.19 billion and RMB15.00 billion were on the Shenzhen Stock Exchange.

1.2 Interest Rate of Bond
In December 2020, average interest rate of issued LGBs was 3.46%, with general bonds
3.40% and special bonds 3.50%. After falling for four consecutive months at the
beginning of 2020 and reaching a year low of 2.91% in April, the issuing interest rate
has continued to rise for six consecutive months since May and then decreased in both
November and December. The average issuing interest rate in December fell by 12 bps
month-on-month and 25 bps year-on-year. The trend of average interest rates of LGBs
issued from January 2019 to December 2020 is shown in Figure 4. Average interest

2 Balance of Local Government Bond
By the end of December 2020, the balance of LGBs was RMB25.49 trillion, with
general bonds RMB12.59 trillion and special bonds RMB12.89 trillion. The average
maturity of outstanding LGBs was 6.9 years, which was 1.9 years longer than that in
the end of 2019. Among them, average maturity of general bonds was 6.3 years and
special bonds 7.5 years. The average interest rate of LGBs was 3.51%, with general
bonds 3.51% and special bonds 3.50%.
The total balance of China’s local government debt was RMB25.66 trillion, including
LGBs and the stock of local government debt in the form of non-government bonds
(175.06 billion), which is under the debt ceiling of RMB28.81 trillion approved by the
National People's Congress.
