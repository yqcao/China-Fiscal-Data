1.3 Interest Rate of Bond
InApril 2022,average interest rate ofissued LGBs was 2.95%, with general bonds 2.89%
and special bonds 2.99%. The average interest rate in April 2022 decreased by 18 bps
month-on-month and 53 bps year-on-year. The yields of bond market showed a general
upward trend in April, the interest rate of issued LGBs dropped significantly mainly
because the proportion of LGBs issued this month with maturity of 10 years and more
was only 54%, so that the average maturity decreased 3.5 years compared with the first
quarter. The trend of average interest rates of LGBs issued from January 2021 to April
2022 is shown in Figure 6. Average interest rates of LGBs issued in April 2022 by
