China Local Government Bond Market Report
(October, 2021)
Issued by China Government Debt Center in November 2021
In October, the issuance of local government bonds (LGBs) were RMB876.06 billion,
with a month-on-month increase of RMB138.22 billion and a year-on-year increase of
RMB433.13 billion. Average interest rate of issued LGBs was 3.35%, with a month-
on-month increase of 11 bps and a year-on-year decrease of 32 bps. Average maturity
of issued LGBs was 13.47 years, with a month-on-month increase of 0.01 years and a
year-on-year decrease of 0.03 years. The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the
secondary market was RMB536.24 billion.
From January to October, the total issuance of LGBs were RMB6491.55 billion.
Average interest rate of issued LGBs was 3.37%, with a decrease of 3 bps year-on-year.
Average maturity of issued LGBs was 11.58 years, with a year-on-year decrease of 3.31
years. The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the secondary market was
RMB6405.00 billion.

1.3 Interest Rate of Bond
In October 2021, average interest rate of issued LGBs was 3.35%, with general bonds
3.15% and special bonds 3.42%. The average interest rate in October 2021 increased
by 11 bps month-on-month and decreased by 32 bps year-on-year. From January to June,
the average interest rate has fluctuated within a narrow range between 3.37% and 3.48%,
mainly because the monetary market interest rate has been relatively stable. Under the
background of the People's Bank of China cutting the reserve requirement ratio in July,
11

the interest rate of issued LGBs decreased significantly in the third quarter. And the
market liquidity continued to remain reasonably abundant with prudent monetary
policy in August and September. The interest rate rose in October, mainly due to the
weakening of easy monetary policy expectations and the supply pressure of LGBs. The
trend of average interest rates of LGBs issued from January 2020 to October 2021 is
