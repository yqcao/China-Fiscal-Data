rate. The trend of average interest rates of LGBs issued from January 2022 to January

3 Balance of Local Government Bond
By the end of January 2023, the balance of LGBs was RMB35.54 trillion, with general
bonds RMB14.39 trillion and special bonds RMB21.15 trillion. The average maturity of
outstanding LGBs was 8.5 years, flat with that in the end of 2022.Among them, average
maturity of general bonds was 6.2 years and special bonds 10.1 years. The average
interest rate ofLGBs was 3.38%, with general bonds 3.38%and special bonds 3.39%.
The total balance of China’s local government debt was RMB35.70 trillion, including
LGBs and the stock of local government debt in the form of non-government bonds
(RMB162.27billion).
