with a month-on-month decrease of RMB603.19 billion and a year-on-year decrease of
RMB710.15 billion. Average interest rate of issued LGBs was 2.24%, with a
month-on-month increase of 9 bps and a year-on-year decrease of 68 bps. Average
maturity of issued LGBs was 15.53 years, with a month-on-month increase of 0.53 years
and a year-on-year increase of 5.84 years. The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the
| secondary | market was | RMB1058.52billion. |     |     |     |

interest rate of issued LGBs was 2.33%, with a year-on-year decrease of 57 bps. Average
maturity of issued LGBs was 13.37 years, with a year-on-year increase of 0.79 years. The
amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the secondary market was RMB15042.44billion.
