1 Market Overview
1.1 Bond Issuance
In August 2020, 31 provinces, autonomous regions, municipalities, separately listed
cities1in China issued 315 local government bonds(LGBs), worth a total of RMB1.20
trillion, as is shown in Table 1.
By bond type, the issuance of general bonds were RMB479.79 billion and that of
special bonds were RMB719.93 billion.
By bond nature, the issuance of new bonds were RMB920.86 billion and that of
refinancing bonds were RMB278.87 billion.
By issuing place, bonds issued in China Central Depository & Clearing Co., Ltd.
(CCDC) were RMB701.11 billion, including RMB2.33 billion of special bonds issued
over commercial bank counters. Bonds issued on the Shanghai Stock Exchange were
RMB351.01 billion and RMB147.60 billion were on the Shenzhen Stock Exchange.

1.5 Trade of Bond in the Secondary Market

RMB1.79 trillion. The amount of bonds traded in the inter-bank market was
RMB1.78trillion, and that on the Shanghai Stock Exchange and the Shenzhen Stock

average turnover rate of LGBs in August 2020 was 3.48‰.
