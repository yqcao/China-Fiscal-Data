1 Market Overview
1.1 Bond Issuance
In January 2020, 26 provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities in China
issued 276 local government bonds, worth a total of RMB785.06 billion.
By bond type, the issuance of general bonds were RMB70.24 billion and that of
special bonds were RMB714.82 billion.
By bond nature, all issued bonds were new bonds, worth a total of RMB785.06
billion.
By issuing place, bonds issued in the inter-bank market were RMB186.28 billion.
Bonds issued on the Shanghai Stock Exchange were RMB436.52 billion and
RMB162.27 billion were on the Shenzhen Stock Exchange.

2 Balance of Local Government Bond
By the end of January 2020, the balance of Chinese local government bonds was
RMB21.90 trillion. In addition, the stock of local government debt (not in terms of
bond) was RMB188.93 billion. Therefore, the total balance of China’s local
government debt was RMB22.09 trillion.
By bond type, the balance of general bonds was RMB11.78 trillion and that of special
bonds was RMB10.12 trillion by the end of January 2020.
By maturity of bond, the average maturity of issued local government bonds was 5.21
years, with general bonds 4.86 years and special bonds 5.61 years by the end of
January 2020.
In terms of interest rate, the average interest rate of local government bonds was
3.54%, with general bonds 3.54% and special bonds 3.53% by the end of January
2020.
