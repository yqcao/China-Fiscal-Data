In April, the issuance of local government bonds (LGBs) were RMB343.91 billion, with
a month-on-month decrease of RMB285.54 billion and a year-on-year decrease of
RMB328.94 billion. Average interest rate of issued LGBs was 2.41%, with a
month-on-month decrease of 5 bps and a year-on-year decrease of 59 bps. Average
maturity of issued LGBs was 11.64 years, with a month-on-month decrease of 1.17 years
and a year-on-year decrease of 1.41 years. The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the
| secondary | market was | RMB1443.84 | billion. |     |     |
| --------- | ---------- | ---------- | -------- | --- | --- |
From January to April, the total issuance of LGBs were RMB1917.77 billion. Average
interest rate of issued LGBs was 2.51%, with a year-on-year decrease of 56 bps. Average
maturity of issued LGBs was 12.51 years, with a year-on-year decrease of 2.07 years.
The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the secondary market was RMB6110.41
billion.
