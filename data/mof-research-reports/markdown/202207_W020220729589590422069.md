China Local Government Bond Market Report
(June,2022)
Issued by ChinaGovernment Debt Center inJuly2022
In June, the issuance of local government bonds (LGBs) were RMB1933.66 billion, with
a month-on-month increase of RMB725.99 billion and a year-on-year increase of
RMB1138.79 billion. Average interest rate of issued LGBs was 3.08%, with a
month-on-month increase of 4 bps and a year-on-year decrease of 38 bps. Average
maturity of issued LGBs was 14.77 years, with a month-on-month increase of 1.79 years
and a year-on-year increase of 2.56 years. The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the
secondary market was RMB855.58billion.
From January to June, the total issuance of LGBs were RMB5250.16 billion. Average
interest rate of issued LGBs was 3.08%, with a decrease of 36 bps year-on-year.Average
maturity of issued LGBs was 14.12 years, with a year-on-year increase of 3.72 years. The
amount ofspot transaction of LGBs inthe secondary market was RMB5005.45billion.

1.3 Interest Rate of Bond

and special bonds 3.14%. The average interest rate in June 2022 increased by 4 bps
month-on-month and decreased by 38 bps year-on-year. The interest rate of issued LGBs
rose month-on-month mainly because the average maturity of issued LGBs increased by
1.79 years month-on-month and the yields of bond market showed a general upward
trend in June. The trend of average interest rates of LGBs issued from January 2021 to
