In December, the issuance of local government bonds (LGBs) were RMB91.46 billion,
with a month-on-month decrease of RMB155.43 billion and a year-on-year decrease of
RMB226.44 billion. Average interest rate of issued LGBs was 2.77%, with a
month-on-month decrease of 6 bps and a year-on-year decrease of 37 bps. Average
maturity of issued LGBs was 6.08 years, with a month-on-month decrease of 3.85 years
and a year-on-year decrease of 2.95 years. The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the
| secondary | market was | RMB728.07billion. |     |     |     |
| --------- | ---------- | ----------------- | --- | --- | --- |
From January to December, the total issuance of LGBs were RMB7367.58 billion.
Average interest rate of issued LGBs was 3.02%, with a decrease of 34 bps year-on-year.
Average maturity of issued LGBs was 13.21 years, with a year-on-year increase of 1.26
years. The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the secondary market was
RMB9612.23billion.
