month-on-month increase of RMB82.51 billion and a year-on-year decrease of
RMB452.32 billion. Average interest rate of issued LGBs was 2.85%, with a
month-on-month decrease of 15 bps and a year-on-year decrease of 19 bps. Average
maturity of issued LGBs was 12.54 years, with a month-on-month decrease of 0.51 years
and a year-on-year decrease of 0.44 years. The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the
| secondary | market was | RMB981.18billion. |     |     |

From January to May, the total issuance of LGBs were RMB3537.88 billion. Average
interest rate of issued LGBs was 3.02%, with a year-on-year decrease of 5 bps. Average
maturity of issued LGBs was 14.14 years, with a year-on-year increase of 0.4 years. The
amount ofspot transaction of LGBs inthe secondary market was RMB4527.99billion.
