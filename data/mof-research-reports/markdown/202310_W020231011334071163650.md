China Local Government Bond Market Report
(August,2023)
Issued byChina Government Debt Center in September 2023
In August, the issuance of local government bonds (LGBs) were RMB1315.94 billion,
with a month-on-month increase of RMB696.80 billion and a year-on-year increase of
RMB925.00 billion. Average interest rate of issued LGBs was 2.74%, which was down
3 bps year-on-year and flat with last month.Average maturity of issued LGBs was 12.55
years, with a month-on-month increase of 2.45 years and a year-on-year increase of 4.00
years. The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the secondary market was
RMB1316.22 billion.
From January toAugust, the total issuance of LGBs were RMB6303.18 billion. Average
interest rate of issued LGBs was 2.91%, with a year-on-year decrease of 14 bps.Average
maturity of issued LGBs was 13.23 years, with a year-on-year decrease of 0.23 years.
The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the secondary market was RMB7827.88
billion.
