China Local Government Bond Market Report
(February,2024)
IssuedbyChina GovernmentDebt Centerin March 2024
In February, the issuance of local government bonds (LGBs) were RMB559.96 billion,
with a month-on-month increase of RMB175.51 billion and a year-on-year decrease of
RMB16.18 billion. Average interest rate of issued LGBs was 2.57%, with a
month-on-month decrease of 3 bps and a year-on-year decrease of 56 bps. Average
maturity of issued LGBs was 15.32 years, with a month-on-month increase of 6.62 years
and a year-on-year decrease of 0.2 years. The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the
secondarymarket wasRMB1336.58billion.
From January to February, the total issuance of LGBs were RMB944.41 billion. Average
interest rate of issued LGBs was 2.58%, with a year-on-year decrease of 54 bps. Average
maturity of issued LGBs was 12.62years, with a year-on-year decrease of 3.48years. The
amountof spottransaction of LGBs inthe secondarymarket wasRMB3059.06billion.
