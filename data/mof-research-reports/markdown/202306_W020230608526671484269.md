China Local Government Bond Market Report
(April,2023)
Issued byChina Government Debt Center inMay 2023
InApril,theissuance oflocal government bonds (LGBs) were RMB672.85billion,with a
month-on-month decrease of RMB217.19 billion and a year-on-year increase of
RMB388.64 billion. Average interest rate of issued LGBs was 3.00%, with a
month-on-month decrease of 5 bps and a year-on-year increase of 5 bps. Average
maturity of issued LGBs was 13.05 years, with a month-on-month decrease of 0.6 years
and a year-on-year increase of 1.87 years. The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the
secondary market was RMB966.57billion.
From January to April, the total issuance of LGBs were RMB2782.52 billion. Average
interest rate of issued LGBs was 3.07%, with a year-on-year decrease of 2 bps. Average
maturity of issued LGBs was 14.58 years, with a year-on-year increase of 0.41 years. The
amount ofspot transaction of LGBs inthe secondary market was RMB3546.80billion.
