secondary market was RMB1.39 trillion. The amount of bonds traded in the

bonds in the secondary market was RMB3.98 trillion. The amount of bonds traded in
the inter-bank market was RMB3.94 trillion, and that on the Shanghai Stock
Exchange and the Shenzhen Stock Exchange were RMB35.37 billion and RMB1.73
