with a month-on-month decrease of RMB540.83 billion and a year-on-year increase of
RMB474.02 billion. Average interest rate of issued LGBs was 2.81%, with a
month-on-month increase of 7 bps and a year-on-year increase of 7 bps.Average maturity
of issued LGBs was 12.51 years, with a month-on-month decrease of 0.04 years and a
year-on-year increase of 2.63 years. The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the
| secondary | market was | RMB973.74billion. |     |     |     |
| --------- | ---------- | ----------------- | --- | --- | --- |
From January to September, the total issuance of LGBs were RMB7078.29 billion.
Average interest rate of issued LGBs was 2.90%, with a year-on-year decrease of 13 bps.
Average maturity of issued LGBs was 13.15 years, with a year-on-year decrease of 0.14
years. The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the secondary market was
RMB8801.62billion.
