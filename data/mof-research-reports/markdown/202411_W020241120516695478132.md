In September, the issuance of local government bonds (LGBs) were RMB1289.32 billion,
with a month-on-month increase of RMB80.20 billion and a year-on-year increase of
RMB514.22 billion. Average interest rate of issued LGBs was 2.15%, with a
month-on-month decrease of 9 bps and a year-on-year decrease of 66 bps. Average
maturity of issued LGBs was 15.00 years, with a month-on-month increase of 0.78 years
and a year-on-year increase of 2.49 years. The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the
| secondary | market was | RMB1618.74billion. |     |     |
| --------- | ---------- | ------------------ | --- | --- |
From January to September, the total issuance of LGBs were RMB6702.08 billion.
Average interest rate of issued LGBs was 2.34%, with a year-on-year decrease of 56 bps.
Average maturity of issued LGBs was 13.15 years, flat with the last year. The amount of
spot transaction of LGBs in the secondary market was RMB13983.92billion.
