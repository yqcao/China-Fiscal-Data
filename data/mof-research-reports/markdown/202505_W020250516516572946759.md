a month-on-month decrease of RMB326.93 billion and a year-on-year increase of

month-on-month increase of 16 bps and a year-on-year decrease of 37 bps. Average
maturity of issued LGBs was 17.33 years, with a month-on-month decrease of 0.09 years
and a year-on-year increase of 4.52 years. The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the
| secondary | market was | RMB2377.77 | billion. |     |     |

maturity of issued LGBs was 17.19 years, with a year-on-year increase of 4.49 years. The
amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the secondary market was RMB5296.77 billion.
