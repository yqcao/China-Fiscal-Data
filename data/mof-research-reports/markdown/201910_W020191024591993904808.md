1Market Overview
1.1 Bond Issuance
In August, 2019, 24 provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities in China
issued 138 local government bonds, worth a total of RMB569.47 billion, increased by
2.4% month-on-month.
By bond type, the issuance of general bonds were RMB163.22 billion and the
issuance of special bonds were RMB406.25 billion.
By bond nature, the issuance of new bonds were RMB342.10 billion and the issuance
of refinancing bonds were RMB167.05 billion. The issuance of swap bonds were
RMB60.31 billion.
By issuing place, bonds issued in the inter-bank market were RMB186.72 billion.
Bonds issued on the Shanghai Stock Exchange were RMB240.24 billion and RMB
142.51 billion were on the Shenzhen Stock Exchange.
