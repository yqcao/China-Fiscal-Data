China Local Government Bond Market Report
(July,2023)
Issued byChina Government Debt Center inAugust 2023
In July, the issuance of local government bonds (LGBs) were RMB619.14 billion, with a
month-on-month decrease of RMB211.09 billion and a year-on-year increase of
RMB212.83 billion. Average interest rate of issued LGBs was 2.74%, with a
month-on-month decrease of 6 bps and a year-on-year decrease of 23 bps. Average
maturity of issued LGBs was 10.10 years, with a month-on-month decrease of 2.63 years
and a year-on-year increase of 0.38 years. The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the
secondary market was RMB908.43 billion.
From January to July, the total issuance of LGBs were RMB4987.24 billion. Average
interest rate of issued LGBs was 2.95%, with a year-on-year decrease of 12 bps.Average
maturity of issued LGBs was 13.40 years, with a year-on-year decrease of 0.4 years. The
amount ofspot transaction of LGBs inthe secondary market was RMB6511.66 billion.
