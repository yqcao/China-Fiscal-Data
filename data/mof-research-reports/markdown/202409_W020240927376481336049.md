month-on-month increase of RMB39.40 billion and a year-on-year increase of

month-on-month decrease of 10 bps and a year-on-year decrease of 50 bps. Average
maturity of issued LGBs was 10.17 years, with a month-on-month decrease of 1.71 years
and a year-on-year increase of 0.07 years. The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the
| secondary | market was | RMB1555.14billion. |     |     |

interest rate of issued LGBs was 2.42%, with a year-on-year decrease of 53 bps. Average
maturity of issued LGBs was 12.27 years, with a year-on-year decrease of 1.13 years.
The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the secondary market was RMB10717.22
billion.
