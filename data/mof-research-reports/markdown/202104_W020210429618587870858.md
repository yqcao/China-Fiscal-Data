China Local Government Bond Market Report
(March, 2021)
Issued by China Government Debt Center in April 2021
In March, the issuance of local government bonds (LGBs) were RMB477.05 billion,
with an increase of RMB421.33 billion month-on-month and RMB89.53 billion year-
on-year. Average interest rate of issued LGBs was 3.44%, with a month-on-month
decrease of 1 bp and a year-on-year increase of 25 bps. Average maturity of issued
LGBs was 7.70 years, with a decrease of 1.17 years month-on-month and 9.86 years
year-on-year. The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the secondary market was
RMB540.41 billion. From January to March, the total issuance of LGBs were
RMB895.11 billion. Average interest rate of issued LGBs was 3.41%, with an increase
of 3 bps year-on-year. Average maturity of issued LGBs was 7.89 years, with a year-
on-year decrease of 7.85 years. The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the
secondary market was RMB1224.49 billion.

By the end of March 2021, the balance of LGBs was RMB26.03 trillion, with general
bonds RMB12.90 trillion and special bonds RMB13.13 trillion. The average maturity
of outstanding LGBs was 6.7 years, which was 0.2 years shorter than that in the end of
2020. Among them, average maturity of general bonds was 6.2 years and special bonds
7.3 years. The average interest rate of LGBs was 3.51%, with general bonds 3.52% and
special bonds 3.51%.
The total balance of China’s local government debt was RMB26.21 trillion, including
LGBs and the stock of local government debt in the form of non-government bonds
(RMB175.06 billion), which is under the debt ceiling of RMB33.28 trillion approved
by the National People's Congress.
