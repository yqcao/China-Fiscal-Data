China Local Government Bond Market Report
(March,2024)
IssuedbyChina GovernmentDebt CenterinApril 2024
In March, the issuance of local government bonds (LGBs) were RMB629.45billion, with
a month-on-month increase of RMB69.49 billion and a year-on-year decrease of
RMB260.59 billion. Average interest rate of issued LGBs was 2.46%, with a
month-on-month decrease of 11 bps and a year-on-year decrease of 59 bps. Average
maturity of issued LGBs was 12.81 years, with a month-on-month decrease of 2.51 years
and a year-on-year decrease of 0.84years. The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the
secondarymarket wasRMB1607.52billion.
From January to March, the total issuance of LGBs were RMB1573.86 billion. Average
interest rate of issued LGBs was 2.53%, with a year-on-year decrease of 56 bps. Average
maturity of issued LGBs was 12.70years, with a year-on-year decrease of 2.36years. The
amountof spottransaction of LGBs inthe secondarymarket wasRMB4666.58billion.
