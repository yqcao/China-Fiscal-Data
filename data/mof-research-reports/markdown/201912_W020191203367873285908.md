1Market Overview
1.1 Bond Issuance
In October, 2019, 9 provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities in China issued
14 local government bonds, worth a total of RMB96.46 billion.
By bond type, the issuance of general bonds were RMB79.58 billion and that of
special bonds were RMB16.88 billion.
By bond nature, the issuance of refinancing bonds were RMB82.37 billionand that of
swap bonds were RMB14.09 billion.
By issuing place, bonds issued in the inter-bank market were RMB14.13 billion.
Bonds issued on the Shanghai Stock Exchange were RMB28.91 billion and
RMB53.42 billion were on the Shenzhen Stock Exchange.

In terms of issuance speed, local governments in China totally issued 1,070 bonds
from January to October in 2019, worth a total of RMB4.28 trillion. Among them,
general bonds were RMB1.75 trillion and special bonds were RMB2.53 trillion. By
bond nature, new bonds were RMB3.04 trillion (exclude loans transferred from
foreign debt, the same as follows), refinancing bonds were RMB1.10trillion and swap
bonds were RMB142.92 billion.

2 Balance of Local Government Bond
By the end of October 2019, the balance of Chinese local government bonds was
RMB21.16 trillion. In addition, the stock of local government debt (not in terms of
bond)was RMB225.04 billion. Therefore, the total balance of China’s local
government debt was RMB21.38 trillion, which is under the debt ceiling of
RMB24.08 trillion approved by the National People's Congress (NPC).
By bond type, the balance of general bonds was RMB11.75 trillion and that of special
bonds was RMB9.41 trillion by the end of October 2019.
By maturity of bond, the average maturity of issued local government bonds was 5.06
years, with general bonds 4.98 years and special bonds 5.17 years by the end of
October 2019.
In terms of interest rate, the average interest rate of local government bonds was
3.54%, with general bonds 3.54% and special bonds 3.53% by the end of October
2019.
