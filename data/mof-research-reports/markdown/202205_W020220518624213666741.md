In March, the issuance of local government bonds (LGBs) were RMB618.67 billion, with
a month-on-month increase of RMB111.57 billion and a year-on-year increase of
RMB141.62 billion. Average interest rate of issued LGBs was 3.14%, with a
month-on-month decrease of 2 bps and a year-on-year decrease of 30 bps. Average
maturity of issued LGBs was 14.72 years, with a month-on-month decrease of 0.35 years
and a year-on-year increase of 7.02 years. The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the
| secondary | market was | RMB829.78billion. |     |     |     |
| --------- | ---------- | ----------------- | --- | --- | --- |
From January to March, the total issuance of LGBs were RMB1824.62 billion. Average
interest rate of issued LGBs was 3.11%, with a decrease of 30 bps year-on-year.Average
maturity of issued LGBs was 14.64 years, with a year-on-year increase of 6.75 years. The
amount ofspot transaction of LGBs inthe secondary market was RMB2619.25billion.
