China Local Government Bond Market Report
(May,2026)
Issued by China Government Debt Center in July 2026
In May, the issuance of local government bonds (LGBs) were RMB804.74 billion, with a
month-on-month decrease of RMB6.56 billion and a year-on-year increase of RMB25.30
billion. Average interest rate of issued LGBs was 1.96%, with a month-on-month
decrease of 2 bps and a year-on-year increase of 9 bps. Average maturity of issued LGBs
was 13.50 years, with a month-on-month increase of 0.38 years and a year-on-year
decrease of 0.66 years. The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the secondary market
was RMB1878.83 billion.
From January to May, the total issuance of LGBs were RMB4721.90 billion. Average
interest rate of issued LGBs was 2.10%, with a year-on-year increase of 15 bps. Average
maturity of issued LGBs was 15.53 years, with a year-on-year decrease of 0.89 years.
The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the secondary market was RMB9274.41
billion.
