1 Market Overview
1.1 Bond Issuance
In December, 2019, 4 provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities in China
issued 7 local government bonds, worth a total of RMB37.99 billion.
By bond type, the issuance of general bonds were RMB3.99 billion and that of special
bonds were RMB34.00 billion.
By bond nature, the issuance of new bonds were RMB20.67 billion and that of
refinancing bonds were RMB2.32 billion and swap bonds RMB15.00 billion.
By issuing place, bonds issued in the inter-bank market were RMB15.00 billion.
Bonds issued on the Shanghai Stock Exchange were RMB1.75 billion and RMB21.24
billion were on the Shenzhen Stock Exchange.

2 Balance of Local Government Bond
By the end of December 2019, the balance of Chinese local government bonds was
RMB21.12 trillion. In addition, the stock of local government debt (not in terms of
bond) was RMB188.93 billion. Therefore, the total balance of China’s local
government debt was RMB21.31 trillion, which is under the debt ceiling of
RMB24.08 trillion approved by the National People's Congress (NPC).
By bond type, the balance of general bonds was RMB11.71 trillion and that of special
bonds was RMB9.40 trillion by the end of December 2019.
By maturity of bond, the average maturity of issued local government bonds was 4.97
years, with general bonds 4.88 years and special bonds 5.09 years by the end of
December 2019.
