1 Market Overview
1.1 Bond Issuance
In September, 2019, 17 provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities in China
issued 90 local government bonds, worth a total of RMB219.58 billion.
By bond type, the issuance of general bonds were RMB56.57 billion and the issuance
of special bonds were RMB163.02 billion.
By bond nature, the issuance of new bonds were RMB141.63 billion (exclude loans
transferred from foreign debt, the same as follows) and the issuance of refinancing
bonds were RMB41.95 billion. The issuance of swap bonds were RMB36.00 billion.
By issuing place, bonds issued in the inter-bank market were RMB57.62 billion,
including RMB500.00 million special bonds issued through commercial bank
counters. Bonds issued on the Shanghai Stock Exchange were RMB88.99 billion and
RMB72.97 billion were on the Shenzhen Stock Exchange.
