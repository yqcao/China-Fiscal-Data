China Local Government Bond Market Report
(January,2024)
IssuedbyChina GovernmentDebt Centerin February2024
In January, the issuance of local government bonds (LGBs) were RMB384.45 billion,
with a month-on-month increase of RMB188.86 billion and a year-on-year decrease of
RMB259.04 billion. Average interest rate of issued LGBs was 2.60%, with a
month-on-month decrease of 23 bps and a year-on-year decrease of 52 bps. Average
maturity of issued LGBs was 8.70 years, with a month-on-month decrease of 1.49 years
and a year-on-year decrease of 7.92years. The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the
secondarymarket wasRMB1722.48billion.

3Balance of Local Government Bond
By the end of January 2024, the balance of LGBs was RMB40.83 trillion, with general
bonds RMB15.87 trillion and special bonds RMB24.96 trillion. The average maturity of
outstanding LGBs was 9.1 years, flat with that in the end of 2023. Among them, average
maturity of general bonds was 6.2 years and special bonds 10.9 years. The average
interestrate ofLGBs was3.27%, with generalbonds3.26% and specialbonds3.27%.
The total balance of China’s local government debt was RMB41.00 trillion, including
LGBs and the stock of local government debt in the form of non-government bonds
(RMB166.22billion).
