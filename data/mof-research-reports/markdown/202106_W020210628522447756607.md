China Local Government Bond Market Report
(May, 2021)
Issued by China Government Debt Center in June 2021
In May, the issuance of local government bonds (LGBs) were RMB875.34 billion, with
a month-on-month increase of RMB99.52 billion and a year-on-year decrease of
RMB427.12 billion. Average interest rate of issued LGBs was 3.43%, with a month-
on-month decrease of 5 bps and a year-on-year increase of 22 bps. Average maturity of
issued LGBs was 11.17 years, with a month-on-month increase of 0.61 years and a year-
on-year decrease of 3.61 years. The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the
secondary market was RMB747.76 billion.
From January to May, the total issuance of LGBs were RMB2546.28 billion. Average
interest rate of issued LGBs was 3.44%, with an increase of 17 bps year-on-year.
Average maturity of issued LGBs was 9.83 years, with a year-on-year decrease of 5.37
years. The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the secondary market was
RMB2798.63 billion.
