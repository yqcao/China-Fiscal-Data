In August, the issuance of local government bonds (LGBs) were RMB390.94 billion,
with a month-on-month decrease of RMB15.37 billion and a year-on-year decrease of
RMB488.79 billion. Average interest rate of issued LGBs was 2.77%, with a
month-on-month decrease of 20 bps and a year-on-year decrease of 45 bps. Average
maturity of issued LGBs was 8.55 years, with a month-on-month decrease of 1.17 years
and a year-on-year decrease of 4.04 years. The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the
| secondary | market was | RMB1010.55billion. |     |     |     |
| --------- | ---------- | ------------------ | --- | --- | --- |
From January toAugust, the total issuance of LGBs were RMB6047.41 billion. Average
interest rate of issued LGBs was 3.05%, with a decrease of 34 bps year-on-year.Average
maturity of issued LGBs was 13.46 years, with a year-on-year increase of 2.51 years. The
amount ofspot transaction of LGBs inthe secondary market was RMB6662.24billion.
