with a month-on-month decrease of RMB433.84 billion and a year-on-year decrease of
RMB433.49 billion. Average interest rate of issued LGBs was 2.83%, with a
month-on-month decrease of 12 bps and a year-on-year decrease of 55 bps. Average
maturity of issued LGBs was 9.93 years, with a month-on-month decrease of 4.67 years
and a year-on-year decrease of 6.9 years. The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the
| secondary | market was | RMB800.43billion. |     |     |     |

Average interest rate of issued LGBs was 3.02%, with a decrease of 35 bps year-on-year.
Average maturity of issued LGBs was 13.3 years, with a year-on-year increase of 1.23
years. The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the secondary market was
RMB8884.16billion.
