China Local Government Bond Market Report
(January, 2022)
Issued by China Government Debt Center in February 2022
In January, the issuance of local government bonds (LGBs) were RMB698.86 billion,
with a month-on-month increase of RMB380.96 billion and a year-on-year increase of
RMB336.52 billion. Average interest rate of issued LGBs was 3.05%, with a month-
on-month decrease of 9 bps and a year-on-year decrease of 32 bps. Average maturity of
issued LGBs was 14.25 years, with a month-on-month increase of 5.22 years and a
year-on-year increase of 6.26 years. The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the
secondary market was RMB1111.53 billion.

3 Balance of Local Government Bond
By the end of January 2022, the balance of LGBs was RMB30.98 trillion, with general
bonds RMB13.73 trillion and special bonds RMB17.25 trillion. The average maturity
of outstanding LGBs was 7.8 years, which was flat with that in the end of 2021. Among
them, average maturity of general bonds was 6.2 years and special bonds 9.1 years. The
average interest rate of LGBs was 3.50%, with general bonds 3.50% and special bonds
3.50%.
The total balance of China’s local government debt was RMB31.14 trillion, including
LGBs and the stock of local government debt in the form of non-government bonds
(RMB162.26 billion).
