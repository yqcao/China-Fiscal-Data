China Local Government Bond Market Report
(June, 2021)
Issued by China Government Debt Center in July 2021
In June, the issuance of local government bonds (LGBs) were RMB794.87 billion,
with a month-on-month decrease of RMB80.48 billion and a year-on-year increase of
RMB508.19 billion. Average interest rate of issued LGBs was 3.46%, with a
month-on-month increase of 3 bps and a year-on-year increase of 23 bps. Average
maturity of issued LGBs was 12.21 years, with a month-on-month increase of 1.04
years and a year-on-year decrease of 1.86 years. The amount of spot transaction of
LGBs in the secondary market was RMB842.75 billion.
From January to June, the total issuance of LGBs were RMB3341.14 billion. Average
interest rate of issued LGBs was 3.44%, with an increase of 17 bps year-on-year.
Average maturity of issued LGBs was 10.40 years, with a year-on-year decrease of
4.71 years. The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the secondary market was
RMB3641.38 billion.

3 Balance of Local Government Bond
By the end of June 2021, the balance of LGBs was RMB27.41 trillion, with general
bonds RMB13.34 trillion and special bonds RMB14.07 trillion. The average maturity
of outstanding LGBs was 7.2 years, which was 0.3 years longer than that in the end of
2020. Among them, average maturity of general bonds was 6.4 years and special
bonds 7.9 years. The average interest rate of LGBs was 3.52%, with general bonds
3.52% and special bonds 3.52%.
The total balance of China’s local government debt was RMB27.58 trillion, including
LGBs and the stock of local government debt in the form of non-government bonds
(RMB175.06 billion), which is under the debt ceiling of RMB33.28 trillion approved
by the National People's Congress.
