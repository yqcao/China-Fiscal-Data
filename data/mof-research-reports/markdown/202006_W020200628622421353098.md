1 Market Overview
1.1 Bond Issuance
In May 2020, 34 provinces, autonomous regions, municipalities, separately listed
cities1 and Xinjiang Production and Construction Corps(XPCC) in China issued 389
local government bonds(LGBs), worth a total of RMB1.3 trillion.
By bond type, the issuance of general bonds were RMB271.49 billion and that of
special bonds were RMB1.03 trillion.
By bond nature, the issuance of new bonds were RMB1.04 trillion and that of
refinancing bonds were RMB262.65 billion.
By issuing place, bonds issued in China Central Depository & Clearing Co., Ltd.
(CCDC) were RMB889.45 billion. Bonds issued on the Shanghai Stock Exchange
were RMB300.38 billion and RMB112.62 billion were on the Shenzhen Stock
Exchange.

1.5 Trade of Bond in the Secondary Market
In May 2020, the amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the secondary market was
RMB1.47 trillion. The amount of bonds traded in the inter-bank market was RMB1.47
trillion, and that on the Shanghai Stock Exchange and the Shenzhen Stock Exchange
were RMB4.88 billion and RMB0.06 billion respectively. The daily average turnover
rate of LGBs in May 2020 was 3.49‰.
From January to May in 2020, the amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the
secondary market was RMB5.34 trillion. The amount of bonds traded in the
inter-bank market was RMB5.3 trillion, and that on the Shanghai Stock Exchange and
