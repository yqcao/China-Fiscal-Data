China Local Government Bond Market Report
(August,2024)
Issued by China Government Debt Center in September 2024
In August, the issuance of local government bonds (LGBs) were RMB1209.12 billion,
with a month-on-month increase of RMB498.29 billion and a year-on-year decrease of
RMB106.82 billion. Average interest rate of issued LGBs was 2.24%, flat with the last
month and a year-on-year decrease of 50 bps. Average maturity of issued LGBs was
14.22 years, with a month-on-month increase of 4.05 years and a year-on-year increase of
1.67 years. The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the secondary market was
RMB1647.96 billion.
From January to August, the total issuance of LGBs were RMB5412.75 billion. Average
interest rate of issued LGBs was 2.38%, with a year-on-year decrease of 53 bps. Average
maturity of issued LGBs was 12.70 years, with a year-on-year decrease of 0.53 years.
The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the secondary market was RMB12365.18
billion.
