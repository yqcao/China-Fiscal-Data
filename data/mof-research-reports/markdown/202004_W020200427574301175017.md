1 Market Overview
1.1 Bond Issuance
In March 2020, 23 provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities in China issued
93 local government bonds, worth a total of RMB387.52 billion.
By bond type, the issuance of general bonds were RMB233.33 billion and that of
special bonds were RMB154.20 billion.
By bond nature, the issuance of new bonds were RMB319.40 billion and that of
refinancing bonds were RMB68.13 billion.
By issuing place, all bonds were issued in China Central Depository & Clearing Co.,
Ltd. (CCDC), worth a total of RMB387.52 billion.

1.2 Interest Rate of Bond
In March 2020, average interest rate of issued local government bonds was 3.19%,
with general bonds 3.26% and special bonds 3.08%. Since the beginning of this year,
the average interest rate has dropped for three consecutive months. In March, it was
down by 52 basis points (bps), 31 bps and 15 bps, compared with that in December,
2019, January and February, 2020, respectively. It was 12 bps down from the lowest
level of 3.31% in February, 2019.The trend of average interest rates of local
government bonds issued from January 2019 to March 2020 are shown in Figure 5.

2 Balance of Local Government Bond
By the end of March 2020, the balance of Chinese local government bonds was
RMB22.63 trillion. In addition, the stock of local government debt (not in terms of
bond) was RMB188.93 billion. Therefore, the total balance of China’s local
government debt was RMB22.82 trillion.
By bond type, the balance of general bonds was RMB12.17 trillion and that of special
bonds was RMB10.47 trillion by the end of March 2020.
By maturity of bond, the average residual maturity of local government bonds was
5.52 years, 5.24 years for general bonds and 5.85 years for special bonds by the end of
March 2020.
In terms of interest rate, the average interest rate of local government bonds was
3.53%, with general bonds 3.54% and special bonds 3.52% by the end of March 2020.
