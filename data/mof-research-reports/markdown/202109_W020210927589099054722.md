China Local Government Bond Market Report
(August, 2021)
Issued by China Government Debt Center in September 2021
In August, the issuance of local government bonds (LGBs) were RMB879.73 billion,
with a month-on-month increase of RMB222.95 billion and a year-on-year decrease of
RMB319.99 billion. Average interest rate of issued LGBs was 3.22%, with a month-
on-month decrease of 9 bps and a year-on-year decrease of 31 bps. Average maturity of
issued LGBs was 12.59 years, with a month-on-month increase of 1 years and a year-
on-year decrease of 2.43 years. The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the
secondary market was RMB733.04 billion.
From January to August, the total issuance of LGBs were RMB4877.65 billion. Average
interest rate of issued LGBs was 3.39%, with an increase of 5 bps year-on-year. Average
maturity of issued LGBs was 10.95 years, with a year-on-year decrease of 4.09 years.
The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the secondary market was RMB5206.60
billion.

1.3 Interest Rate of Bond
In August 2021, average interest rate of issued LGBs was 3.22%, with general bonds
3.05% and special bonds 3.28%. The average interest rate in August 2021 decreased by
9 bps month-on-month and 31 bps year-on-year. From January to June, the average
interest rate has fluctuated within a narrow range between 3.37% and 3.48%, mainly
because the monetary market interest rate has been relatively stable. Under the
background of the People's Bank of China cutting the reserve requirement ratio in July,

3 Balance of Local Government Bond
By the end of August 2021, the balance of LGBs was RMB28.30 trillion, with general
bonds RMB13.44 trillion and special bonds RMB14.85 trillion. The average maturity
of outstanding LGBs was 7.4 years, which was 0.5 years longer than that in the end of
2020. Among them, average maturity of general bonds was 6.4 years and special bonds
8.3 years. The average interest rate of LGBs was 3.52%, with general bonds 3.51% and
special bonds 3.52%.
The total balance of China’s local government debt was RMB28.47 trillion, including
LGBs and the stock of local government debt in the form of non-government bonds
(RMB175.06 billion), which is under the debt ceiling of RMB33.28 trillion approved
by the National People's Congress.
