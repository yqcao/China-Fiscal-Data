China Local Government Bond Market Report
(September, 2021)
Issued by China Government Debt Center in October 2021
In September, the issuance of local government bonds (LGBs) were RMB737.84 billion,
with a month-on-month decrease of RMB141.88 billion and a year-on-year increase of
RMB17.30 billion. Average interest rate of issued LGBs was 3.24%, with a month-on-
month increase of 2 bps and a year-on-year decrease of 40 bps. Average maturity of
issued LGBs was 13.46 years, with a month-on-month increase of 0.87 years and a
year-on-year decrease of 1.26 years. The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the
secondary market was RMB662.16 billion.
From January to September, the total issuance of LGBs were RMB5615.49 billion.
Average interest rate of issued LGBs was 3.37%, with a decrease of 1 bps year-on-year.
Average maturity of issued LGBs was 11.28 years, with a year-on-year decrease of 3.72
years. The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the secondary market was
RMB5868.76 billion.

1.3 Interest Rate of Bond
In September 2021, average interest rate of issued LGBs was 3.24%, with general bonds
3.03% and special bonds 3.29%. The average interest rate in September 2021 increased
by 2 bps month-on-month and decreased by 40 bps year-on-year. From January to June,
the average interest rate has fluctuated within a narrow range between 3.37% and 3.48%,
mainly because the monetary market interest rate has been relatively stable. Under the
background of the People's Bank of China cutting the reserve requirement ratio in July,
11

the interest rate of issued LGBs decreased significantly in the third quarter. And the
market liquidity continued to remain reasonably abundant with prudent monetary
policy in August and September. The trend of average interest rates of LGBs issued
from January 2020 to September 2021 is shown in Figure 6. Average interest rates of
LGBs issued in September 2021 by maturity are shown in Table 5.
