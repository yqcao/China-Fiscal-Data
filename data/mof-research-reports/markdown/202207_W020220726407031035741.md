In May, the issuance of local government bonds (LGBs) were RMB1207.67 billion, with
a month-on-month increase of RMB923.47 billion and a year-on-year increase of
RMB332.33 billion. Average interest rate of issued LGBs was 3.04%, with a
month-on-month increase of 9 bps and a year-on-year decrease of 39 bps. Average
maturity of issued LGBs was 12.98 years, with a month-on-month increase of 1.8 years
and a year-on-year increase of 1.81 years. The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the
| secondary | market was | RMB900.20billion. |     |     |
| --------- | ---------- | ----------------- | --- | --- |
From January to May, the total issuance of LGBs were RMB3316.50 billion. Average
interest rate of issued LGBs was 3.07%, with a decrease of 37 bps year-on-year.Average
maturity of issued LGBs was 13.74 years, with a year-on-year increase of 3.91 years. The
amount ofspot transaction of LGBs inthe secondary market was RMB4149.87billion.
