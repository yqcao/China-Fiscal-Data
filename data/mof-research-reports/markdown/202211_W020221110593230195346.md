with a month-on-month decrease of RMB89.86 billion and a year-on-year decrease of
RMB436.76 billion. Average interest rate of issued LGBs was 2.74%, with a
month-on-month decrease of 3 bps and a year-on-year decrease of 50 bps. Average
maturity of issued LGBs was 9.88 years, with a month-on-month increase of 1.33 years
and a year-on-year decrease of 3.58 years. The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the
| secondary | market was | RMB768.19billion. |     |     |     |     |

Average interest rate of issued LGBs was 3.03%, with a decrease of 34 bps year-on-year.
Average maturity of issued LGBs was 13.29 years, with a year-on-year increase of 2.01
years. The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the secondary market was
RMB7430.43billion.
