China Local Government Bond Market Report
(August,2025)
IssuedbyChinaGovernmentDebtCenterinSeptember2025
In August 2025, the issuance of local government bonds (LGBs) were RMB980.14
billion, with a month-on-month decrease of RMB233.36 billion and a year-on-year
decrease of RMB228.99 billion.Average interest rate of issued LGBs was 2.04%, with a
month-on-month increase of 19 bps and a year-on-year decrease of 20 bps. Average
maturity of issued LGBs was 14.35 years, with a month-on-month decrease of 0.20 years
and a year-on-year increase of 0.13 years. The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the
secondary market was RMB1520.23 billion.
From January to August, the total issuance of LGBs were RMB7683.79 billion. Average
interest rate of issued LGBs was 1.92%, with a year-on-year decrease of 46 bps.Average
maturity of issued LGBs was 15.47 years, with a year-on-year increase of 2.77 years. The
amount of spot transaction of LGBs inthe secondary market was RMB14132.24 billion.
