month-on-month decrease of 5 bps and a year-on-year decrease of 58 bps. Average
maturity of issued LGBs was 14.16 years, with a month-on-month decrease of 1.64 years
and a year-on-year increase of 0.47 years. The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the
| secondary | market was | RMB1540.68 | billion. |     |     |

maturity of issued LGBs was 16.42 years, with a year-on-year increase of 3.53 years. The
amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the secondary market was RMB8783.39 billion.
