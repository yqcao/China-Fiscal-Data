In June, the issuance of local government bonds (LGBs) were RMB830.23 billion, with a
month-on-month increase of RMB74.87 billion and a year-on-year decrease of
RMB1103.43 billion. Average interest rate of issued LGBs was 2.80%, with a
month-on-month decrease of 5 bps and a year-on-year decrease of 28 bps. Average
maturity of issued LGBs was 12.73 years, with a month-on-month increase of 0.19 years
and a year-on-year decrease of 2.04 years. The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the
| secondary | market was | RMB1075.24 | billion. |     |     |

interest rate of issued LGBs was 2.98%, with a year-on-year decrease of 10 bps.Average
maturity of issued LGBs was 13.87 years, with a year-on-year decrease of 0.25 years.
The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the secondary market was RMB5603.23
billion.

1.3 Interest Rate of Bond

central bank's interest rate cut, the interest rates of issued LGBs decreased this month.
