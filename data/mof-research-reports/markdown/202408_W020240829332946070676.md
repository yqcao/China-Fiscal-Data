month-on-month decrease of RMB232.16 billion and a year-on-year decrease of
RMB158.79 billion. Average interest rate of issued LGBs was 2.34%, with a
month-on-month decrease of 11 bps and a year-on-year decrease of 46 bps. Average
maturity of issued LGBs was 11.88 years, with a month-on-month decrease of 1.81 years
and a year-on-year decrease of 0.85 years. The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the
| secondary | market was | RMB1685.01billion. |     |     |     |

interest rate of issued LGBs was 2.46%, with a year-on-year decrease of 52 bps. Average
maturity of issued LGBs was 12.69 years, with a year-on-year decrease of 1.18 years.
The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the secondary market was RMB9162.08
billion.
