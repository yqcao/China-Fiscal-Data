month-on-month increase of 1 bp and a year-on-year decrease of 3 bps.Average maturity
of issued LGBs was 15.52 years, with a month-on-month decrease of 1.1 years and a
year-on-year increase of 0.45 years. The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the
