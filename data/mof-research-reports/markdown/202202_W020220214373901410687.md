China Local Government Bond Market Report
(December, 2021)
Issued by China Government Debt Center in January 2022
In December, the issuance of local government bonds (LGBs) were RMB317.90 billion,
with a month-on-month decrease of RMB362.48 billion and a year-on-year increase of
RMB134.28 billion. Average interest rate of issued LGBs was 3.14%, with a month-
on-month decrease of 24 bps and a year-on-year decrease of 32 bps. Average maturity
of issued LGBs was 9.03 years, with a month-on-month decrease of 7.8 years and a
year-on-year increase of 0.86 years. The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the
secondary market was RMB775.61 billion.
From January to December, the total issuance of LGBs were RMB7489.83 billion.
Average interest rate of issued LGBs was 3.36%, with a decrease of 4 bps year-on-year.
Average maturity of issued LGBs was 11.95 years, with a year-on-year decrease of 2.7
years. The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the secondary market was
RMB8069.36 billion.

1.3 Interest Rate of Bond
In December 2021, average interest rate of issued LGBs was 3.14%, with general bonds
2.94% and special bonds 3.17%. The average interest rate in December 2021 decreased
by 24 bps month-on-month and decreased by 32 bps year-on-year. In this year, the
market liquidity remained reasonably abundant and the average interest rate of issued
LGBs was relatively stable in the first half of the year. The average interest rate in the
second half of the year decreased especially in the third quarter and the end of year,
under the background of the People's Bank of China cutting the reserve requirement
ratio in July and December. The average interest rate reached the lowest of the year in
December, further because the average maturity was shorter. The trend of average
interest rates of LGBs issued from January 2020 to December 2021 is shown in Figure
6. Average interest rates of LGBs issued in December 2021 by maturity are shown in
Table 5.
