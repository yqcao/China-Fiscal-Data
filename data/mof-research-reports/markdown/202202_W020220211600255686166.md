China Local Government Bond Market Report
(November, 2021)
Issued by China Government Debt Center in December 2021
In November, the issuance of local government bonds (LGBs) were RMB680.38 billion,
with a month-on-month decrease of RMB195.68 billion and a year-on-year increase of
RMB542.02 billion. Average interest rate of issued LGBs was 3.38%, with a month-
on-month increase of 3 bps and a year-on-year decrease of 20 bps. Average maturity of
issued LGBs was 16.83 years, with a month-on-month increase of 3.36 years and a
year-on-year increase of 4.54 years. The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the
secondary market was RMB888.75 billion.
From January to November, the total issuance of LGBs were RMB7171.93 billion.
Average interest rate of issued LGBs was 3.37%, with a decrease of 3 bps year-on-year.
Average maturity of issued LGBs was 12.07 years, with a year-on-year decrease of 2.77
years. The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the secondary market was
RMB7293.75 billion.

1.3 Interest Rate of Bond
In November 2021, average interest rate of issued LGBs was 3.38%, with general bonds
3.06% and special bonds 3.41%. The average interest rate in November 2021 increased
by 3 bps month-on-month and decreased by 20 bps year-on-year. From January to June,
the average interest rate has fluctuated within a narrow range between 3.37% and 3.48%,
mainly because the monetary market interest rate has been relatively stable. Under the
background of the People's Bank of China cutting the reserve requirement ratio in July,
the interest rate of issued LGBs decreased significantly in the third quarter. And the
market liquidity continued to remain reasonably abundant with prudent monetary
policy in August and September. The interest rate rose in October, mainly due to the
weakening of easy monetary policy expectations and the supply pressure of LGBs. The
interest rate rose slightly in November, mainly because the proportion of bonds with
maturity of 10 years and more increased. The trend of average interest rates of LGBs
issued from January 2020 to November 2021 is shown in Figure 6. Average interest
rates of LGBs issued in November 2021 by maturity are shown in Table 5.
