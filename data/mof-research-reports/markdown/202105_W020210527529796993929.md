China Local Government Bond Market Report
(April, 2021)
Issued by China Government Debt Center in May 2021
In April, the issuance of local government bonds (LGBs) were RMB775.82 billion, with
an increase of RMB298.77 billion month-on-month and RMB489.06 billion year-on-
year. Average interest rate of issued LGBs was 3.48%, with an increase of 4 bps month-
on-month and 57bps year-on-year. Average maturity of issued LGBs was 10.56 years,
with a month-on-month increase of 2.86 years and a year-on-year decrease of 3.51 years.
The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the secondary market was RMB826.38
billion.
From January to April, the total issuance of LGBs were RMB1670.93 billion. Average
interest rate of issued LGBs was 3.44%, with an increase of 13 bps year-on-year.
Average maturity of issued LGBs was 9.13 years, with a year-on-year decrease of 6.36
years. The amount of spot transaction of LGBs in the secondary market was
RMB2050.87 billion.
