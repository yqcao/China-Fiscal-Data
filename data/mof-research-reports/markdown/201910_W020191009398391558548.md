1 Market Overview
1.1 Bond Issuance
In July, 2019, 27 provinces, autonomous regions and municipalities in China issued
177 local government bonds, worth a total of RMB555.94 billion, decreased by 38.2%
month-on-month.
By bond type, the issuance of general bonds were RMB161.98 billion and the
issuance of special bonds were RMB393.96 billion.
By bond nature, the issuance of new bonds were RMB376.52 billion and the issuance
of refinancing bonds were RMB158.37 billion. The issuance of swap bonds were
RMB21.05 billion.
By issuing place, bonds issued in the inter-bank market were RMB118.76 billion
(including RMB800 million special bonds issued through commercial bank counters).
Bonds issued on the Shanghai Stock Exchange were RMB356.39 billion and RMB
80.79 billion were on the Shenzhen Stock Exchange.

2 Balance of Local Government Bond
By the end of July 2019, the balance of Chinese local government bonds was
RMB20.67 trillion. In addition, the stock of local government debt (not in terms of
bond) was RMB390.81 billion. Therefore, the total balance of China’s local
government debt was RMB21.06 trillion in the first seven months of 2019, which is
under the debt ceiling of RMB24.08 trillion approved by the National People's
Congress (NPC).
By bond type, the balance of general bonds was RMB11.73 trillion and that of special
bonds was RMB8.95 trillion by the end of July 2019.
By maturity of bond, the average maturity of issued local government bonds was 4.90
years, with general bonds 4.87 years and special bonds 4.94 years by the end of July
2019.
In terms of interest rate, the average interest rate of local government bonds was
3.52%, with general bonds 3.52% and special bonds 3.53% by the end of July 2019.
